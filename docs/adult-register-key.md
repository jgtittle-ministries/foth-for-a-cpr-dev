# The Adult Register Key

*How to read the seeded series — which were written for a family room at a school — as the adult leadership year, until each file's adult rewrite lands. Where a seeded page and this key disagree, this key and the [Leadership Year Handbook](leadership-year-handbook.md) govern.*

## The two register rules

**Rule one: convert room roles, keep life roles.** The same word can name a seat in the room or a fact of a member's life, and only the seat converts. *Teen*, *parent*, and *family* as seats in the room become *cohort member* and *the cohort*. But an adult cohort member still goes home to a real household — so where a session is about a member's actual family (the household week, the practices that go home to kitchen tables, the Four Questions), the family language stands, because the life is the point.

**Rule two: the covering replaces the school.** Every institutional hook transfers from the school to the host church: approval, authority, policy, premises, and the door out. Nothing loses its covering; the covering changes address.

## One conversion is a ruling, not a reading

The family edition's engine is **parents tell first**: a parent who goes first with something true licenses a depth the teen would never volunteer cold. The adult year needs that engine transposed, and the natural candidate is **the leader tells first** — the convening leader, and then whoever leads each session, going first with something real, licensing the room the same way. The weekly Feedback Round already runs on this logic; the question is whether the *telling* practices (story weeks, confession, one true sentence) formally adopt leader-first as their stated engine. That is a design ruling, not a vocabulary swap, and it is flagged in the backlog for John's call. Until it is ruled, leaders should simply do it — go first, with something true — and note what it does to the room.

## What this key does not cover

The session scripts themselves still speak family-edition language in their examples, watch-fors, and contingencies. Reading them through this key works for planning; running a session well means the leader has made the conversions before the room arrives. The per-file adult rewrite is queued, series by series, Getting Started first.
