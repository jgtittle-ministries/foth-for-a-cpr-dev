## C. Design questions John must rule

16. **The therapy rail for adults — RULED 2026-08-20.** The settled design:
    - **The rail is identical in the adult edition.** In the room, sessions may be therapeutic and are never therapy — no looser than the family edition, because the leadership year is the template year: leaders run what they lived.
    - **Some deepening inside the rail is approved** (John's ruling). Adult sessions — Going Deeper's knots week especially — may go further than the family edition in examples and fullness of the work, staying on the rail's near side.
    - **The deeper blockage work is a named door, not a session.** The intense-knots territory the prototype found needed ("work to help us be clearer and hear clearer") is real and the year says so — and points to it. Optional, self-chosen, one-on-one or otherwise outside the group room, never required, never counted.
    - **Who may facilitate that level of work is the host church's call, not this curriculum's** (John's ruling, grounded in the established Formation Companion principle: this project is not a certifying body). The host church qualifies its Formation Companion as capable of this level of work, and the host church holds spiritual authority and accountability over the FC. The curriculum's job is to name the level of work and the question the church must answer — clearly enough that no church can miss that it *is* a question.
    - **The single-gender consideration is mentioned, not ruled** (John's ruling). The traditions behind the deepest knot work often run single-gender; the curriculum names the issue and leaves the call to the host church.
    - Proposed and standing unless John objects: the knots week *teaches* the rail as a leader competency (recognizing the line, referring warmly across it), and anything beyond the sessions is explicitly marked *personal formation, not program template*.
    - Retrofit landing sites when the pass reaches them: GD week-03, the adult handbook's safety section, the door-out/referral architecture in adult dress, and a cross-reference to the FC chapter for the not-a-certifying-body principle.
17. **The seriousness gate — RULED 2026-08-20.** The settled design: three gates, and only the first looks like one.
    - **Entry gate — the real seriousness test, and the only demands in the design (none of them on an individual):** (a) the covering's blessing, formally given — the pastor or elders commission the cohort and take spiritual authority over it before session one (RULED: hard requirement); (b) the calendar committed — the whole year on the church's calendar before it starts; (c) the covenant signed, where the year is observed. A church that will not do these three has answered its own question at the door, at no cost to anyone.
    - **The year itself is the gate — read, never scored** (RULED: no attendance threshold, no published targets; attrition is data, read honestly the way the prototype's own shrinking was). What the reading holds: whether the team finished together, who led and received the round, what survived the holds, and whether the covenant ever cost anything — a covenant that never cost anything was not real.
    - **Rotation is a self-discerned (and observed) role, not a requirement** (RULED). Session-leadership in the family year emerges from self-discernment plus what the year observed. A cohort member who never led is not failed; roles differ, and the family year needs more than session-leaders.
    - **Exit = discernment plus rite, not a certificate.** A structured discernment — the cohort's own keep/change/tell, the covering's read, the year's evidence on the table — ending in the **host church's** decision to proceed to the family edition; then a commissioning rite, deliberately unmeasured, mirroring the club year's ending. **FotH may offer to help witness the discernment; the discernment and its consequences belong to the host church** (RULED — the not-a-certifying-body principle, same as item 16).
    - Because the materials are published under CC BY-SA, the gate cannot and does not gate access. It gates **relationship**: counsel, fellowship with the pilot family of churches, the name said with blessing. Nothing is demanded; the church gates itself.
    - Landing sites when the retrofit reaches them: the adult handbook (the entry conversation and the exit discernment, written out), start-here (the offer's plain terms), and eventually the CPR side of the bridge — how a church that read CPR takes up the challenge.
